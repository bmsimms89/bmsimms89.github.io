About Simmons Glass

Three generations of craftsmanship on South Whidbey.

Established in 1967 by Carl Simmons, Simmons Glass, Inc. has served generations of South Whidbey homeowners through glasswork, residential construction, and a reputation earned project by project.

Our story

Family-owned and community-rooted.

Simmons Glass, Inc. was established in 1967 by Carl Simmons. What began with one generation has continued through three, with the Simmons family serving South Whidbey homeowners for nearly 60 years.

Carl’s son, Kenon Simmons, took over the business more than 30 years ago, continuing the company’s work in glass and residential construction. Brandon grew up around the business, working alongside both his grandfather and father beginning in his middle-school years. After graduating college in 2013, he became more involved in the company and has continued carrying the family business forward.

Today, Simmons Glass focuses primarily on premium shower enclosures, window retrofits, and replacement glass, backed by decades of residential construction experience. While the work has changed over the years, the approach has remained straightforward: careful workmanship, honest service, and work we’re proud to put the Simmons name on.

Trusted local craftsmanship, premium glasswork, and residential building experience serving Langley, Clinton, Freeland, and Greenbank.
